Bring your criteria to X.

  1. Download and load

    Unzip the extension. At chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Select the folder that contains manifest.json.

  2. Define what matters

    Open settings, paste the goal and profile into their fields, add your own TypeSafe key, enable automatic judgment, and save. The extension UI is currently Chinese; the English guide translates the controls.

  3. Browse as usual

    The extension marks loaded posts with icons and percentages. Optionally compare eligible posts to get up to three top matches.

What makes a post worth reading?

A rule should be reusable and open to challenge. Contribute a reading goal, a criterion, or a counterexample that exposes where a rule fails.

Data flow and costs

This sample does not access your X account or collect an API key. The installed extension sends loaded post text, author handles, engagement counts, and your goal/profile to TypeSafe. Your key is stored in local extension storage; provider API charges may apply. It does not automatically search, scroll, or like posts. A match score is not a measure of truth or accuracy.
